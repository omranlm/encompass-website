Who We Are

A Voice for Freedom & Open Society

EnCompass e.V. is a registered non-profit association based in Berlin, Germany. We are committed to spreading the concepts and values of liberalism and personal freedoms — within our society and far beyond its borders.

We believe that open, free societies flourish when individuals are empowered through access to culture, knowledge, and free expression. Our work brings together artists, academics, journalists, and engaged citizens who share this conviction.

What We Do

Our Work

We pursue our mission through four core areas of activity, each designed to strengthen open societies and amplify the voices that sustain them.

🎨

Art & Culture

We support artistic projects and cultural events that champion freedom of expression and bring liberal ideas to life through creative work.

📡

Media & Content

We produce and support content across print, digital, and broadcast formats to broaden public understanding of liberal values and open society.

🎓

Workshops & Seminars

We organize educational events — open to practitioners, academics, students, and the public — that foster critical thinking and civic engagement.

✍️

Authors & Academics

We support writers, researchers, and scholars whose work advances knowledge of free and open societies — providing platforms, funding, and networks.
